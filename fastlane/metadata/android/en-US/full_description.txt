<b>PlainApp</b> is a free, open-source Android app that turns your phone into a self-hosted management hub accessible from any web browser on the same network - no accounts, no cloud, no subscriptions.

<b>Why PlainApp?</b>

Most phone-management tools route your data through their own servers, lock features behind subscriptions, or show ads. PlainApp is different:

<ul>
<li><b>100% local</b> - your data never leaves your network</li>
<li><b>End-to-end encrypted</b> - every connection is secured with TLS + XChaCha20-Poly1305</li>
<li><b>Free and ad-free</b> - no purchases, no upsells, ever</li>
<li><b>Fully open-source</b> - audit the code, build your own binary, or contribute</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>

<b>Web-Based Phone Management</b>
Open a browser on any device connected to the same Wi-Fi network to manage your Android phone - no app installation required on the computer side.

<ul>
<li><b>Files</b> - Browse internal storage, SD card, and USB drives; upload, download, rename, delete</li>
<li><b>Media</b> - View and manage photos, videos, and audio; stream them in the browser</li>
<li><b>Contacts</b> - View, search, and manage contacts</li>
<li><b>SMS &amp; Calls</b> - Read messages and call logs; send SMS from your desktop</li>
<li><b>Apps</b> - List installed apps and export APKs</li>
<li><b>Notifications</b> - Mirror phone notifications to the browser in real time</li>
<li><b>Screen Mirror</b> - Live-stream the phone screen to the browser with audio and remote control support</li>
<li><b>Device Info</b> - Check battery, storage, network, and system details</li>
</ul>

<b>Built-in Android Tools</b>
PlainApp is also a capable standalone app:

<ul>
<li><b>Markdown Notes</b> - Write and organize notes with a clean editor</li>
<li><b>RSS Reader</b> - Follow your favorite websites with a distraction-free reader</li>
<li><b>Video &amp; Audio Player</b> - Full-featured media player with playlist support</li>
<li><b>TV Cast</b> - Cast media to any DLNA/Chromecast-compatible TV</li>
<li><b>Peer-to-Peer Chat &amp; File Sharing</b> - Share files and messages directly between devices without a server</li>
<li><b>Pomodoro Timer</b> - Stay focused with a built-in work timer</li>
<li><b>Sound Meter</b> - Measure ambient noise levels in real time</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy &amp; Security</b>

<ul>
<li>No Firebase Analytics or Messaging - crash reporting is optional and opt-in</li>
<li>Zero data sent to any third-party server</li>
<li>All traffic between phone and browser is encrypted</li>
<li>No account registration required</li>
</ul>
